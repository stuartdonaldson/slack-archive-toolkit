# Prompt: F3 Puget Sound Regional Newsletter

Generate a concise F3 Puget Sound regional newsletter from the supplied Slack digest and profile exports.

Use only the supplied Slack data. Do not invent details.

## Goal

Give PAX a short regional pulse covering what matters across Puget Sound and nearby regional workspaces without requiring them to read every Slack channel.

Prioritize:

- major regional announcements
- regional leadership transitions
- CSAUPs and cross-region events
- unusual or destination workouts
- 2.0 / family events
- service opportunities
- AO launches, closures, relocations, or major schedule changes
- major community outcomes
- broadly useful calls to action

Do not elevate routine administration, ordinary backblasts, individual onboarding, or narrow AO/channel activity unless it clearly matters beyond that immediate group.

## Candidate-completeness guard

Before drafting, build an internal candidate inventory from all current-period Slack data.

A story is a **presumptive candidate** if any of these are true:

- explicitly described as regional, cross-region, all-PAX, or open to multiple regions
- promoted in more than one regional workspace
- invites PAX beyond the host AO, or invites Ms / 2.0s / FNGs
- is a CSAUP, destination workout, unusual-format workout, family event, service event, regional challenge, major celebration, leadership transition, or significant AO change



Merge reposts into the canonical/original announcement, but do not lose the underlying event.

For every presumptive candidate, make an explicit include/omit decision before drafting. Omit only for a specific reason such as cancellation, duplication, staleness, insufficient actionable detail, or genuinely narrow relevance.

**Do not use brevity as a reason to silently drop a qualifying story. Shorten prose before reducing coverage. There is no per-region story quota.**

Before finalizing, verify that every presumptive candidate is either included or has a defensible omission reason.

- **For upcoming events, check for recency as well as existence.** If the latest organizer/canonical update is materially old relative to the event date and key logistics remain unresolved, do not present the event as fully current or actionable.
- **When an upcoming event has no recent confirming update, qualify it explicitly** — e.g. “still listed for [date], but no recent organizer update/details were found” — or move it to **Items needing clarification** if missing details could affect participation.

## Editorial test

Ask:

**Would a PAX outside the immediate channel reasonably care about this?**

If yes, include it unless there is a clear reason not to.

## Evidence rules

- Prefer canonical/original announcements over reposts.
- Prefer the responsible organizer or leader over secondary mentions.
- Resolve `<@USERID>` using the same-workspace profile export.
- Prefer F3/display names.
- Do not merge identities across workspaces unless strongly supported.
- Use direct Slack `message_url` links whenever available.
- Attribute material items by F3/display name.
- Do not infer that a planned event happened; look for later confirmation.
- If no follow-up confirms completion, describe it as planned, scheduled, or upcoming.
- If details conflict, use the strongest/newest source and briefly note the conflict when material.
- If a material detail is unavailable, say so rather than guessing.

## Voice

Use a concise, practical F3 voice: direct, energetic, community-minded, occasionally playful.

Use F3 language naturally where useful: PAX, gloom, Q, CSAUP, EH, 2.0, coffeeteria, Freed to Lead, iron sharpens iron.

Keep each story focused on:

1. what is happening,
2. why PAX should care,
3. what to do next.

Most stories should be one short paragraph.

## Leadership transitions

Treat a regional leadership transition as one story about the team.

Summarize the transition first, then name key incoming, continuing, or departing leaders explicitly established by the source.

Do not split supporting role changes into separate stories unless independently significant.

Focus on continuity, new leadership, and PAX being Freed to Lead.

Significant outcomes or announcements from roughly the last 2–3 weeks. Omit if empty.

### On deck

Meaningful upcoming items.

Then include only regional sections with meaningful broadly relevant material, alphabetically:

## F3 Cascades

## F3 North Sea

## F3 Redmond

## F3 Seattle

## F3 Tundra

## Cross-region events to know

Use this as a short reminder list for events likely to attract PAX from multiple regions.

Put the full story under the host/canonical region, then only a brief reminder here.

For events, include when available:

- date
- time
- location
- signup/contact link
- useful variants such as pre-ruck, 2.0 option, coffeeteria, alternate start, or separate track

## Items needing clarification

Include only unresolved details that materially affect participation, such as unclear date/time/location, conflicting announcements, missing signup/contact path, or no usable Slack link.

Do not list minor data imperfections.

## Final trim

Before finalizing:

- remove duplicate references
- merge reposts
- cut unnecessary history
- remove narrow administrative detail
- compress repetitive event descriptions
- keep all qualifying broad-interest stories

The finished newsletter should feel like a regional pulse from the gloom: complete enough to trust, concise enough to read.
